Questions researchers ask

Not generic SaaS FAQ — answers about evidence, control, and hallucinations.

How is Dhund different from ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a general chat interface. Dhund is a Research Operating System: you import papers, extract evidence, write with linked citations, and verify claims before export. Writing is bound to research evidence you control — not to an open-ended prompt.

Can Dhund replace my research?

No. Dhund accelerates literature review and drafting while keeping researchers in control. You accept evidence, revise claims, and own the manuscript.

How does Dhund reduce hallucinations?

Drafts are grounded in EvidenceObjects extracted from your Research Ready papers. Citations link back to sources; Research Confidence and reviewer checks surface weak or unsupported claims so you can fix them before publication.

What do I need to get started?

A project, papers in your Library (upload or Zotero/Mendeley), and Research Ready status before evidence extraction. Then write and verify in the Writing workspace.
