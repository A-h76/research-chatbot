Frequently asked questions

Is Dhund another “write my thesis” chatbot?

No. Dhund is a Research Operating System. The point is not one prompt → eighty pages. It’s keeping your library, evidence decisions, and grounded writing in one place so drafts stay tied to sources you accepted.

Where do citations come from?

From papers you import and evidence you accept. Bindings connect manuscript spans to evidence objects, then back to paper metadata for export — including BibTeX when bibliography data is available.

Can AI invent sources I didn’t upload?

Writing is gated on accepted evidence from your project. Candidates don’t silently enter drafts. You still review and revise — Dhund doesn’t replace scholarly judgment.

Who is Dhund for?

Researchers running real literature reviews — MSc/PhD students, supervisors, and labs. Create an account to get started.
