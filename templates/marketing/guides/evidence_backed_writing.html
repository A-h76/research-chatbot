What is evidence-backed writing?

Claims that resolve to sources you accepted.

Evidence-backed writing is draft text where each material claim can be traced to a source the author inspected and endorsed — not merely to a model’s memory.

Grounded vs generated

“Generated” means the model produced prose. “Grounded” means the prose is bound to EvidenceObjects from your Research Ready papers. Only the second is defensible in a thesis committee.

The binding loop

  • Extract candidate evidence from papers
  • Accept or reject each candidate
  • Write with citations linked to accepted evidence
  • Verify confidence and revise weak claims

What it is not

It is not “paste a prompt and receive a chapter.” It is a Research OS workflow: Library → Evidence → Writing → Verification → Export.
