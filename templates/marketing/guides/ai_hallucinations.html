Why AI hallucinates in research

Fluency is not verification.

Language models optimise for plausible continuation. Research requires accountable continuation — sentences that survive citation checks.

Where invention sneaks in

  • Fabricated references that look real
  • Correct-sounding methods attributed to the wrong paper
  • Overconfident synthesis when evidence is thin

What reduces risk

Constrain generation to evidence you imported and accepted. Surface grounding metrics. Force a human review step before export. That is Dhund’s bias: evidence binding over open-ended chat.

What does not fix it

Longer prompts alone. A prettier chat UI. “Trust the model.” Hallucination risk drops when the system refuses to write beyond your corpus and makes gaps visible.
