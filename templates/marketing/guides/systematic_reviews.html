Best practices for systematic reviews

Protocol before prose.

Systematic reviews trade speed for reproducibility. The habits below transfer whether you use PRISMA-style reporting or a structured narrative review.

Protocol first

Register or write down the question, databases, dates, and inclusion criteria before screening. Changing rules mid-stream invites bias.

Dual screening when stakes are high

Two reviewers (or a documented single-reviewer process with audit) reduce missed inclusions and silent exclusions.

Structured extraction

Extract the same fields from every study: design, sample, measures, outcomes, limitations. Dhund’s Evidence step is designed for this discipline.

Bias and confidence

Note risk of bias and strength of evidence explicitly. Verification is not optional decoration — it is how you avoid overselling thin literatures.

Report the trail

Others should reconstruct how you got from search to claim. Export with citations and evidence links is part of that trail.
