Outcomes across the research workflow

Not four features — one pipeline.

Dhund is organised around what researchers need to finish: a defensible manuscript grounded in sources they control.

Library↓ Evidence↓ Writing↓ Verification↓ Export

Library

Import PDFs, connect Zotero or Mendeley, and organise papers into projects until they are Research Ready. Your corpus — not a chat upload.

Evidence

Extract methods, datasets, metrics, and findings into reviewable EvidenceObjects. Accept what you trust; reject what you do not.

Writing

Draft literature review sections with citations bound to accepted evidence — Outline, Manuscript, and Evidence side by side.

Verification

Research Confidence and reviewer checks surface weak or unsupported claims before you ship the draft.

Export

Leave with a manuscript you can defend — citations and evidence trail intact, not a disposable AI essay.
