The workflow, not the pitch

1 · Import PDFs

Bring papers in via upload, Zotero, Mendeley, Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, DOI, PubMed, arXiv, Europe PMC, or ORCID. Dhund stores them in your Library and runs the same analysis pipeline on every source.

2 · Organise Research

Assign papers to projects and collections. Wait for Research Ready before extraction.

3 · Extract Evidence

Run Evidence Extraction to create candidate EvidenceObjects — methods, datasets, metrics, findings — then accept what stands.

4 · Write

Generate literature review sections in the Writing workspace. Citations link to evidence you already accepted.

5 · Verify Claims

Inspect Research Confidence and reviewer issues. Revise until claims are supportable.

6 · Export

Export when the draft is ready for supervisors, journals, or your thesis chapter.
